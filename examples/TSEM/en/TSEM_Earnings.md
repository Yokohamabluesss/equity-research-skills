# TSEM Earnings Call Log

Tower Semiconductor Ltd. (TSEM) | fiscal year = calendar year

## TL;DR
### FY26 Q1 (2026-05-13)
- **Revenue $414M (+15% YoY), net profit $65M (+62% YoY), 16% net margin**; Q2 guided to $455M ± 5% at the midpoint, the highest in the company's history (+22% YoY, +10% QoQ), and management "strongly reiterate[d]" quarter-over-quarter revenue and margin growth through 2026.
- **Silicon photonics (SiPho) revenue tripled YoY** and drives the quarter. Long-term SiPho contracts represent **$1.3B of 2027 revenue (wafers delivered)**, 2028 contracts are "significantly larger", and about **$290M of prepayments** has been received. Management said $1.3B is not its 2027 SiPho forecast: it is "forecasting substantially higher".
- **Capacity**: SiPho capacity grows **5x** from the 2025Q4 shipment base by end-2026; Fab 2 and Fab 7 (Uozu, 300mm) shipped SiPho for the first time, Fab 7 at 95% first-lot yield; after the TPSCo restructuring Tower will own Fab 7 outright, with adjacent land to reach 4x today's size (production 1H28 at the earliest).
- **Technology and new form factors**: all-silicon 400G/lane MZ modulator demonstrated with Coherent; multiple NPO design wins with strong 2027 demand; two XPO demos at OFC on Tower SiPho; CPO is several years out.
- **2028 model may be raised** "within the next quarters"; the 300mm expansion is not in the current model.

### FY26 Q2 (2026-08-04)
- **Revenue $460M, a record (+11% QoQ, +24% YoY), slightly above the $455M guidance midpoint**; gross margin 30%, operating margin 20% and net margin 20%, all company records (excluding one-offs); Q3 guided to $520M at the midpoint, an annualised run rate above $2B.
- **2028 model raised**: revenue $3.6B (+$760M), gross margin 45% (was 39%), net margin 33% (was 26%); the raise comes from Japan Track 1 (Arai fab converted to 300mm SiPho); Track 2 is not in the model.
- **SiPho revenue +60% QoQ, +270% YoY, Q2 annualised run rate above $680M, targeting a $1B run rate in Q4 2026**; Q4 wafer-start capacity will be over 3x Q2 SiPho shipments, with the full financial effect in 2027 Q2.
- **Japan dual track**: Track 1 reaches full production readiness in 2027 Q4 and its new capacity is already "spoken for"; Track 2, a new fab, quadruples Japan 300mm output with all tools in place by 2028 Q4, funded from internal cash with no dilution.
- **The $1.3B 2027 contract figure was not updated this quarter**; NPO is expected to be "tens of percent" of SiPho shipments in 2027 (mostly 2H); a multi-year epi-wafer supply agreement with IQE eases the InP constraint.

### FY26 Q3 (2026-11-02)
- **Revenue $526M, a record (+14% QoQ, +33% YoY), above the $520M guidance midpoint**; gross profit $179M (34%, +92% YoY), operating profit $127M (24%, 2.5x YoY), net profit $120M (23%); incremental gross margin 62%, while the CFO keeps 55–58% for the long term.
- **Q4 guided to $600M at the midpoint**, which gives $2B for 2026 (2025: $1.57B) and a $2.4B annualised run rate; management says 2027 Q1 will show **no seasonal dip** and keep growing.
- **SiPho revenue about $240M in Q3**; the Q4 annualised run rate is expected to **exceed $1.1B** (last quarter's target: $1B); year-end wafer-start capacity is about 2x Q3's, and 3x by 2027 Q3 with Track 1 (about $720M a quarter, a $2.8–2.9B run rate, excluding integrated lasers).
- **Track 1 ahead of schedule**: all tools ordered and slotted, full production starts in 2027 Q3 (last quarter: production readiness in 2027 Q4); **capacity contracts extended to 2029**; all expansion self-funded.
- **NPO prototyping with more than 10 top customers**, appreciable shipments expected in 2H27; a large SiGe capacity agreement with Semtech; NewPhotonics integrated InP lasers in initial production. **The $1.3B contract figure was not mentioned this quarter.**

## Drivers of Profitability
### FY26 Q1
1. **Mix shift to higher-margin SiPho / SiGe**: gross margin up from 20% a year ago to 27%, operating profit +96% YoY.
   > "It's really just selling more newer higher margin products." / "Yes. Yes." (analyst's summary, confirmed by the CFO)
2. **SiPho revenue 3x and growth in every major platform**: image sensors +9%, RF SOI +12%, power management +10%, SiGe +24%.
   > "this year has begun in a very strong fashion, led by silicon photonics with a revenue growth of 3x year-over-year. All major technology offerings demonstrated year-over-year growth"
3. **One-off tax benefit lifts net profit**: 9% tax rate versus 15%+ in the model, from a non-recurring TPSCo income-tax benefit.
   > "The reason for it is the inclusion of a non-recurring income tax benefit recorded for the first quarter of 2026 in relation to TPSCo"
4. **Pricing**: 200mm BCD prices up 13% (a revaluation of the platform, not a capacity-shortage increase).
   > "our 200 mm BCD pricing has increased by 13%"

### FY26 Q2
1. **High drop-through on incremental revenue (mix)**: incremental revenue converted to gross, operating and net profit at 58%, 55% and 55%.
   > "representing respectively 58%, 55%, and 55% quarter-over-quarter contribution from the increased revenue"
2. **RF infrastructure / SiPho ramp**: RF infrastructure is 49% of revenue, +43% QoQ and over +140% YoY; SiPho +60% QoQ, +270% YoY.
   > "Silicon photonics revenue itself increased by over 60% quarter-over-quarter and over 270% year-over-year"
3. **Cost efficiency**: only about 10 points between gross margin and operating margin.
   > "Today, we operate at about a 10 percentage point difference between gross margin and operating margin"
4. **Drags**: 300mm RF SOI −14% YoY (200mm → 300mm move, Fab 7 capacity given to SiPho / SiGe); image sensors roughly flat YoY.

### FY26 Q3
1. **High drop-through on incremental revenue**: revenue +33% YoY, gross profit +92%, operating profit 2.5x, net profit 2.2x; incremental gross margin 62%, partly because only about 65% of the $920M of tools has started depreciating.
   > "still not all the tools started their depreciation schedule"
2. **SiPho ramp**: about $240M of SiPho revenue in Q3, with capacity expanding at four sites.
   > "a business that contributed approximately $240 million of silicon photonics revenue in Q3 2026 alone"
3. **Richer mix**: four of five factories expanding and repurposing toward infrastructure products.
   > "the expansion and repurposing of four of our five factories towards substantially higher infrastructure mix"
4. **Pricing**: several strategic BCD customers signed long-term agreements at higher ASPs, giving a forecast 20% higher blended price in 2027 than 2026.

## Product Breakdown
### Company-wide Demand / Order Book / Capacity Overview
#### FY26 Q1
- **Long-term contracts and prepayments (SiPho customers, company-level visibility)**: 2027 contracts represent $1.3B of revenue, 2028 contracts are "significantly larger", and about $290M of prepayments has been received; this is not these customers' full demand and excludes the 50+ other SiPho customers.
  > "contractually representing $1.3 billion revenue in 2027, with significantly larger valued contracts for 2028, backed by approximately $290 million in prepayments already received from our largest SiPho customers"
  > "these reservations do not represent the entire expressed demand of these customers, nor the extent of our planned shipment to these customers"
- **What $1.3B measures**: wafers delivered in 2027; the company's own 2027 SiPho forecast is "substantially higher".
  > "Wafers delivered in 2027."
  > "That $1.3 billion is not what we're forecasting for SiPho in 2027. ... Forecasting substantially higher."
- **Capacity plan**: the $920M SiGe / SiPho expansion (8-inch fabs in Israel, Newport Beach and San Antonio, plus the 12-inch Uozu fab) is on track; in 2027 the focus moves to Uozu 300mm. After the TPSCo restructuring Tower owns Fab 7 outright; adjacent land allows 4x today's size.
  > "With access to adjacent land, we expect to further build out and scale up to four times current levels"
- **New-fab timing**: tools can move in about 1.5 years after METI approval, "in best case, in the first half of 2028"; meanwhile tools are added in the existing Fab 7 and the Arai building on the TPSCo campus to meet 2027 demand.
  > "Our plan is certainly to meet customers' demand within 2027 while developing meaning building the shell that is substantially big."
- **Utilisation**: Fab 2 ~60% (SiPho / SiGe qualifications under way), Fab 3 80% (slightly constrained while new SiPho / SiGe flows are introduced; recovers in Q2), Fab 5 75%, Fab 7 full (above the 85% model), Fab 9 80%.
- **Raw-material constraint**: indium phosphide (InP) starting material is tight; the company says supply arrangements are in place.
  > "In our case, not necessarily the end laser product is the constraint, the starting material is the constraint."
- **Pricing principle**: no price increases because capacity is tight; premiums come from new platforms.
  > "We are not a company that likes to indiscriminately raise prices because of a capacity constraint."

### Silicon Photonics (SiPho) [optical-related]
#### FY26 Q1
- **Revenue**: 3x YoY; 2025 SiPho revenue about $230M (CEO).
  > "led by silicon photonics with a revenue growth of 3x year-over-year"
- **Capacity**: 5x the 2025Q4 shipment base by end-2026; Fabs 2, 3, 9 and 7 ramping together; Fab 2 and Fab 7 shipped for the first time, Fab 7 at 95% first-lot yield.
  > "Our expansion remains on track to grow SiPho capacity five times from the base of our Q4 2025 wafer revenue shipments by the end of this year, 2026."
##### Demand / Industry Dynamics
1. **200G ramping, older products still in strong demand**:
   > "we continued a strong ramp of 200 Gbps products for multiple customers while continuing to support strong demand in older products"
2. **Port counts rising fast (LightCounting)**: SiPho ports 30M in 2025 → 137M in 2028, total ports 90M → 205M; pluggables remain the main form at least through 2030.
   > "From 2025, the amount of SiPhO ports ... was 30 million, driving to 2028 SiPhO ports to 137 million."
   > "Pluggables will stay extremely strong at least through the 2030."
3. **New form factors: NPO first, XPO extends pluggables, CPO in several years**: multiple NPO design wins and strong 2027 demand; two XPO demos at OFC on Tower SiPho.
   > "We are already seeing strong demand for NPO products in 2027."
   > "NPO is likely to ramp over the next several years and precede a significant ramp in CPO for our primary customers."
4. **Technology differentiation (modulators and integrated lasers)**: all-silicon 400G/lane MZ modulator shown with Coherent; heterogeneous 400G/lane InP EAM on the PH18DA platform with OpenLight; thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) and organic-polymer modulators moving toward production; the CEO expects TFLN to be strong for one generation before InP takes over.
   > "If you look at advanced modulators, the indium phosphide has tremendous advantages on form factor."
   > "I think the lithium niobate will come in fairly strong for one generation. I don't think it will last for many generations. I think it will go to an indium phosphide."
5. **CPO path**: investment in 200mm / 300mm hybrid bonding + TSV, DWDM light sources and small high-performance modulators; the difference from TSMC is III-V modulation, not digital logic.
   > "As far as having modulation with three, five and other material that's much better than anyone else could have, that's where we would add value in that."
6. **Competitive position**: "by far the leading market share" (no number).
   > "I think that we're certainly the leading market share, and by far the leading market share in silicon photonics presently. I see no reason why that should change."
- **Summary**: in the ramp phase - ports 4.5x in three years, lead customers locked in with long-term contracts, form factors extending from pluggables to NPO / XPO.

#### FY26 Q2
- **Revenue**: +60% QoQ, +270% YoY, Q2 annualised run rate above $680M; RF infrastructure (SiPho + SiGe) is 49% of company revenue.
- **Capacity**: Q4 2026 wafer-start capacity more than 3x Q2 SiPho shipments, full financial effect in 2027 Q2; target $1B run rate in Q4 2026.
  > "creating a wafer start capacity over three times higher than the second quarter silicon photonics revenue shipments, with full financial effect anticipated to be in the second quarter of 2027"
##### Demand / Industry Dynamics
1. **From scale-out to scale-up**: 800G–1.6T pluggables have largely replaced copper outside the rack; the next step is optical scale-up, led by NPO.
   > "The next frontier is enabling optical interconnects for scale up, either within a single rack or across multiple racks."
2. **NPO at scale**: several NPO deployments over the next year; "tens of percent" of 2027 shipments.
   > "I think it'll be not in the single digits, but in the tens of the percentage of what we'll be shipping, especially in the second half of the year."
3. **Coherent optics (scale-across)**: SiPho coherent modules with Marvell have passed multi-million units; coherent matters more in campus-spanning AI architectures.
   > "the number of SiPho-based coherent optical modules crossed over into multi-million"
4. **Differentiation: insertion loss and next-generation modulators**: lowest insertion loss can cut the number of CW lasers (InnoLight has published roughly half); 400G modulators pursued in all-silicon, TFLN and InP.
   > "Insertion loss is probably one of the biggest things that one could look for."
5. **III-V heterogeneous integration entering a volume window**: integrated lasers, advanced modulators and optical signal processing go to high volume in the next 1–2 years; integrated lasers bring "several tens of millions" of revenue next year.
   > "Even right now for the integrated laser, for an indium phosphide laser, we would see next year several tens of millions of revenue from integrated laser."
- **Summary**: ramp phase, and the second growth leg (NPO, integrated lasers, 300mm) is already scheduled for 2027.
- **vs prior quarter**: 🔼 from "200G ramp + strong 2027 NPO demand" to "NPO tens of percent of 2027 shipments + integrated lasers tens of millions next year + multi-million coherent modules"; 🆕 scale-up named as the next frontier.
