# Core
The leading silicon photonics foundry, supplying both the optical (SiPho) and electrical (SiGe) chips of a transceiver
	SiPho share "by far the leading" (CEO, no number given); 50+ active SiPho customers
		🔼 RF infrastructure (SiPho + SiGe) is 49% of revenue (FY26 Q2)
		🔼 FY26 Q3: the CEO says majority share of pluggable scale-out has been reached
		⚠️ FY26 Q3: the President acknowledges competition ("room for more than one player"), no share number
	Sells wafers: the 2027 contracts ($1.3B) are measured in wafers delivered; customers are module makers and chip companies (Coherent, NVIDIA and others)
Special traits
	Supply-constrained: revenue follows the capacity ramp; customers lock capacity with long-term contracts and prepayments
	No price increases because capacity is tight; premiums come from new platforms
		⚠️ FY26 Q2: the $1.3B contract figure was not updated; new capacity is "spoken for" but not all booked
		📌 CFO: the wafer selling price is the model's biggest variable
		🔼 FY26 Q3: capacity contracts extended to 2029; ⚠️ the $1.3B figure no longer appears, replaced by "billions of dollars of long-term agreements"
Main growth drivers
	SiPho for 800G / 1.6T pluggable transceivers (AI data-center scale-out)
		SiPho ports 30M in 2025 → 137M in 2028 (LightCounting); pluggables remain the main form at least through 2030
		Capacity 5x the 2025Q4 shipment base by end-2026; revenue 3x YoY
		🔼 Q2 annualised run rate >$680M, targeting $1B in Q4 2026
		🆕 Coherent (scale-across): SiPho coherent modules with Marvell past multi-million units
		🔼 FY26 Q3 ~$240M, Q4 annualised >$1.1B; 2027 Q3 start capacity ~$720M a quarter
	SiGe drivers / TIAs, growing almost 1:1 with transceiver ports
		Demand "unprecedented"; slower growth and lower margin than SiPho
		🔼 Across pluggables, LPO and LRO; 100G / 200G per lane in high volume in three 200mm fabs
		🔼 NPO replaces the DSP with SiGe TIAs / drivers; large capacity agreement with Semtech
New narrative
	NPO: from pluggables only to near-packaged optics for scale-up
		Before → after: pluggables → NPO ramps ahead of CPO (CPO several years out)
		Evidence: multiple design wins, meaningful shipments in 2026–2027, strong 2027 demand
		🔼 Scale-up named the next frontier; NPO "tens of percent" of 2027 SiPho shipments (mostly 2H)
		🔼 FY26 Q3: prototypes with 10+ top customers, appreciable 2H27 shipments (depending on adoption)
	III-V integration: from pure silicon to integrated InP lasers and modulators
		Evidence: InP integrated laser / 400G EAM on the OpenLight platform; all-silicon 400G/lane MZ modulator shown with Coherent
		Stage: integrated lasers about to ramp in 2026; modulators in demo / moving to production
		🔼 Integrated lasers "several tens of millions" of revenue in 2027; InP supply eased by the IQE agreement
		🔼 FY26 Q3: NewPhotonics integrated InP lasers (die-to-wafer) in initial production
	Japan 300mm: full ownership of Fab 7 after the TPSCo restructuring, with land to grow it 4x
		Stage: awaiting METI approval, production 1H28 at the earliest; not in the 2028 model
		🔼 METI support: Track 1 production-ready in 2027 Q4 (capacity spoken for), driving the 2028 model from $2.8B to $3.6B
		🔼 Track 2 new fab quadruples Japan 300mm output, all tools by 2028 Q4; internal cash, no dilution
		🔼 FY26 Q3: Track 1 brought forward to full starts in 2027 Q3; Track 2 buildings in 2028, expansion in 2029
	🆕 Solid-state SiPho OCS: from transceivers into optical switching
		🆕 Evidence: management calls it the "go-to solution", with nanosecond switching and compatibility with the CPO / NPO ecosystem
		🆕 Stage: no revenue or customer count given
